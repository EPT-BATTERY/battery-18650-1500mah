III. Waste Battery Management
1
Description of Hazardous Substances
This battery does not contain mercury, cadmium or lead exceeding the regulatory limits. The lithium-ion electrolyte contained may cause fire when damaged. Do not puncture or throw it into the fire.
Battery collection symbol:
2
Correct Classification and Placement Guidelines
Do not discard in household garbage or mixed garbage.
Please place the used batteries in the designated battery recycling bins or return them to the retailer's recycling points.
Battery Safety Instructions
  1. Don't immerse the battery in water and seawater. Please put it in a cool and dry environment when not in use.
  2. Do not discard or leave the battery near a heat source, such as a fire or heater.
  3. Being charged, using the battery charger specifically for that purpose.
  4. Don't reverse the positive and negative terminals.
  5. Don't connect the battery to an electrical outlet directly.
  6. Don't connect the positive and negative terminals directly with metal objects such as wire. Short terminals of the battery are strictly prohibited, it may damage the battery.
  7. Do not transport and store the battery together with metal objects such as necklaces, hairpins.
  8. Do not strike, throw, or trample the battery.
  9. Do not directly solder the battery or pierce the battery with a nail or other sharp object.
  10. Do not use lithium-ion batteries and other different lithium polymer battery models in mixture.
  11. Prohibition of use of damaged cells.
  12. Don't bend or fold the sealing edge. Don't open or deform the folding edge. Don't fillet the end of the folding edge.
  13. Don't fall, hit, or bend the battery body.
